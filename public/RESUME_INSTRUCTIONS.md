# Resume Setup Instructions

Place your resume PDF file in this directory as `resume.pdf`.

The "View Resume" button in the Hero section links to `/resume.pdf`, which will open your resume in a new browser tab.

## Steps:

1. Export your resume as a PDF
2. Name it exactly: `resume.pdf`
3. Place it in: `public/resume.pdf`
4. The file will be automatically served at the site root when you deploy

**Note:** This folder (`public/`) is served as-is by Vite/Vercel — anything you put here is accessible at your domain root.

Example: If you deploy to `https://sarmadshafiq.vercel.app`, your resume will be at:
`https://sarmadshafiq.vercel.app/resume.pdf`
